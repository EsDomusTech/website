Common inquiries regarding our engagement process and financial structures.

How do you calculate architectural fees?

Our fees are typically calculated as a percentage of the total construction cost, or as a fixed lump sum for specific design phases. We provide a full breakdown after our initial discovery session.

Can we start with Phase 01 and decide later?

Absolutely. Many of our clients prefer to engage us for a Conceptual Study first to define the vision before committing to full-service project delivery.

Are structural engineering costs included?

While we manage the coordination with all consultants, specialized engineering fees are usually billed separately to ensure direct accountability and professional oversight from the specific discipline.
