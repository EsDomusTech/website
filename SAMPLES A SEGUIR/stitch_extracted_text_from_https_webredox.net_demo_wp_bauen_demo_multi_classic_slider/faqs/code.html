01 / DESIGN PROCESS

Our process begins with a comprehensive consultation to understand your lifestyle, aesthetic preferences, and functional needs. We then conduct a site analysis before presenting a conceptual framework that balances structural integrity with artistic vision.

While we strive for perfection in the schematic phase, we understand that vision evolves. Change orders are managed through a formal review process to assess impacts on both budget and structural timelines.

02 / BUDGETING

Fees typically range from 10% to 15% of the total construction cost, depending on the project's complexity and scope of services required—from basic drafting to full-service interior design and project management.

03 / PROJECT TIMELINES

High-end residential projects generally span 18 to 24 months. This includes 4–6 months of design and permitting, followed by 12–18 months of meticulous construction and finishing.
